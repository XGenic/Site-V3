Sandbars

The shallow sandbars off Fort Lauderdale’s coast create a natural oasis where visitors and residents gather to enjoy Florida’s coastal lifestyle. These crystal-clear, sun-warmed waters form naturally through tidal patterns and ocean currents, offering an ideal setting for everything from peaceful swimming to lively gatherings.

Each day, the sandbar tours become a bustling hub of activity, with boats, jet skis, and watercraft anchoring in these accessible waters. This makes it an excellent destination for all boat rentals with a captain.

These boat-accessible venues perfectly capture Fort Lauderdale’s nautical spirit. Our team is always ready to suggest the best waterfront restaurants to enhance your boating adventure.

Atlantic

A captained boat rental in Fort Lauderdale offers prime access to one of the world’s most diverse marine ecosystems. Positioned at the northern edge of the Florida Reef—the planet’s third-largest barrier reef system—these waters teem with over 6,000 distinct marine species. The rich biodiversity makes every moment on the water an opportunity for discovery.

With nearly year-round warm waters, take the chance to use our equipment, or bring your own and snorkel Fort Lauderdale and find the wonders that lay underwater. If you prefer to relax along the pristine waters, that works too; it’s the perfect setting for an unforgettable day at sea.
