Waterfront Dining in Fort Lauderdale

Most of our Fort Lauderdale dinner cruises start at the end of the New River, passing directly through the heart of the city. Take a leisurely river cruise on our bow, and experience downtown Fort Lauderdale's nightlife and historic remnants. From there, enter Millionaires Row and explore the opulent scenery as we pass the most extravagant homes in America.

If opting for our private chef experience, we’ll head north and anchor at one of the many secluded lakes in the Intracoastal Waterway, building the most romantic dinner on a yacht in Fort Lauderdale. Once anchored, we’ll shut the engines off, allowing you to intake the quiet ambiance of the calm waters and starlit night sky.

When ready, enter the cabin with your meal awaiting you, or step off the boat and into any of the Fort Lauderdale restaurants on the water. Enjoy an intimate evening with a private dinner cruise for two or an exciting night with friends and family. With endless options and first-class service, we can accommodate any desires.

Visit a Restaurant on the Water

End a sunset cruise with a stop at one of Fort Lauderdale's best restaurants. There's no better way to show up to the most popular spot in town than with a yacht charter. Build a custom getaway and choose from a selection of first-class dinner spots. With a reservation in place, we'll dock at the restaurant and have a table waiting for your arrival.

Shooters

A laid-back spot right on the Intracoastal Waterway that offers classic American foods, drinks, and sometimes live music. It’s an excellent spot for those who want elevated American cuisine with a twist on seafood. Come for dinner and enjoy a raw bar featuring delicious oysters.

Menu

Mastros Ocean Club

A twist on the classic steakhouse, Mastro's seafood location is recognized for its combination of world-class service, highly acclaimed cuisine, and live entertainment in an elegant yet energetic atmosphere. Whether you’re in the mood for steak or seafood, Mastros has an impressive selection.

Menu

15th Street Fisheries

A long-standing marina eatery dishing upscale & casual fare with live music and tarpon feeding. A great place to eat alongside the water like a local. If you're looking for a less casual vibe, the second floor features a more upscale dining experience with award-winning seafood options.

Menu

Kaluz

A modern American restaurant that offers an extensive menu of fresh seafood, hand-cut steaks, crisp salads, hand-patted burgers, and more. Sit alongside the water and more unique dishes, such as fire-roasted octopus and maple leaf duck confit.
