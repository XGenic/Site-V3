Stranahan House

While far from the most extravagant home found on the river, the Stranahan House contrasts heavily against the modern architecture of downtown Fort Lauderdale. The house is the oldest standing building in Broward County, a product of Frank Stranahan, who built it in 1901 as a trading post and later transformed it into a residence. This historic landmark is a testament to Fort Lauderdale’s humble beginnings, showcasing Florida’s vernacular architecture with its wide porches and raised construction. The building served multiple purposes throughout its history – as a post office, community center, town hall, and now a Fort Lauderdale boat tour attraction – making it not just a house but the very heart of early Fort Lauderdale. Today, the restored structure offers visitors a glimpse into the city’s pioneering days, with its period furnishings and authentic architectural details preserved through careful restoration.

Riverside Hotel

The Riverside Hotel is one of the most influential buildings on the Las Olas landscape. The hotel opened its doors in late 1936, bringing a new level of sophistication to Fort Lauderdale’s burgeoning tourism industry. Started by two brothers from Chicago, the Riverside Hotel has a distinctly Mediterranean flavor to its architecture. This building style would influence the rest of Las Olas Boulevard’s development, which has always been described as distinctly European. The hotel’s iconic tower and elegant façade have become synonymous with Las Olas Boulevard’s prestigious reputation. Throughout its history, the Riverside Hotel has hosted countless celebrities, business tycoons, and distinguished guests, maintaining its position as a symbol of Fort Lauderdale’s golden age of hospitality. The hotel’s recent renovations have carefully preserved its historic charm while incorporating the modern luxuries found throughout the Fort Lauderdale boat tour.

Bahia Mar

Most famously known as the hub for boat shows, this marina holds some of the largest yachts in the world. With some mega yachts nearing $500 million, an up-close view makes for an unforgettable experience. The Bahia Mar is not just a marina; it’s a world-class yachting destination that hosts the renowned Fort Lauderdale International Boat Show, the largest in-water boat show in the world. The marina’s state-of-the-art facilities can accommodate vessels up to 300 feet long, making it a preferred destination for some of the most impressive superyachts ever built. Beyond its remarkable moorage capabilities, Bahia Mar offers a glimpse into the luxury yachting lifestyle with its iconic yacht club, high-end restaurants, and exclusive shopping opportunities. The facility’s rich history dates back to the 1940s, and it has evolved from a military installation into one of the most prestigious marine destinations in the world.

Las Olas Boat Tour

Each trip takes us close to Las Olas Blvd, the vibrant heart of Fort Lauderdale’s social and cultural scene. The hotspot for anyone visiting Fort Lauderdale, Las Olas is the city’s cultural Main Street, filled with an eclectic mix of upscale boutiques, art galleries, and world-class restaurants. Known as “the waves” in Spanish, Las Olas first came about in the 1920s to connect downtown Fort Lauderdale with the beach. With it becoming the eventual mainline for the city, the development of Las Olas began to spike, transforming from a quiet thoroughfare into a sophisticated boulevard. Today, you’ll see a fascinating blend of new and old buildings, restaurants, shops, and events alongside the street as you go up and down the New River, which runs parallel. The boulevard’s Mediterranean revival architecture, lush landscaping, and waterfront charm create the perfect starting point for our tours, offering guests a glimpse into Fort Lauderdale’s historic roots and contemporary luxury lifestyle. Our Fort Lauderdale boat tour route along the New River provides unique perspectives of this famous street’s landmarks.
