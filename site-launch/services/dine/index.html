Shooters Waterfront

A laid-back spot on the Intracoastal Waterway offering classic American food, drinks, live music & water views. It is an excellent choice for those who want high-end American cuisine with a twist on seafood. This waterfront restaurant also makes for a great stop on one of our Fort Lauderdale brunch cruises.

15th Street Fisheries

Close to the Fort Lauderdale Sandbar, this long-standing marina eatery dishes upscale and casual food. With live music, a large bar, and tarpon feeding from the dock, it’s a great time for all ages. If you’re looking to eat like a local with freshly caught seafood, this should top your list.

Hungry Dutchman

A new and convenient staple of the sandbar, the Hungry Dutchman floats on the water with a fully stocked kitchen ready to serve classic American cuisine. With a menu offering hamburgers, hot dogs, and shrimp ceviche, you can keep the party boat going.

Menu

Jay’s Food Boat

Another floating restaurant found at the sandbar, Jay’s offers excellent chicken wings and fries, but also some more Florida-centric offerings such as oysters and alligator bites.

Dinner Options

Being on the water during the later part of the day is a magical experience. As the sun starts to set, the homes begin to illuminate. If you are looking for a unique dinner experience, we have many options available to choose from.

  • Private Chef: Our most unique yacht rental offers a private dinner cruise. Enjoy an intimate evening with a leisure cruise and an award-winning meal made on board by our private chef.
  • Visit a Restaurant: Whether booking a sunset cruise or a custom trip running through the evening, visiting a waterfront restaurant is always an option. The evening hours unlock a new set of upscale menus and options.
  • On-Board Dining: Utilize our Sea Rays large dinette and be served by the waterfront restaurant staff while onboard a yacht.
