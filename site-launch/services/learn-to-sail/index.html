Cruising

Bimini Sailing Charter

Discover the magic of Bimini, the westernmost islands of the Bahamas, just a short sailing charter from Fort Lauderdale. This tropical paradise offers an intimate escape with its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and rich marine ecosystem. Bimini is a haven for adventure seekers and relaxation enthusiasts alike, boasting world-class sport fishing, incredible snorkeling and diving sites, and fascinating historical landmarks like the legendary “Healing Hole” and remnants of presumed Atlantis-related structures. From swimming with wild dolphins to exploring vibrant coral reefs, enjoying local Bahamian cuisine, or simply lounging on powdery white sands, Bimini provides an extraordinary mix of natural beauty, marine life, and cultural experiences that make every moment of your Fort Lauderdale to Bahamas charter truly unforgettable.
