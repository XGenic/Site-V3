Fort Lauderdale Activity

Fort Lauderdale is unsurprisingly a top travel destination for most. Its location along the warm Atlantic waters and close proximity to other popular destinations make it one of the best places to visit in South Florida. If you want to make the most of your stay here, spend a day on a yacht with a fully customizable itinerary. Whether looking for adventure or a leisure cruise, we offer options suitable for all.

As you travel down the river, you’ll explore the heart of our downtown. With an interesting mix of waterfront parks, restaurants, and modern architecture, it’s an eye-catching part of all Fort Lauderdale day trips. Along with these modernities, you’ll catch a glimpse of the past with landmarks such as the Stranahan House, an old turn-of-the-century trading outpost and currently the oldest standing building in Broward County.

The downtown gradually transitions into residential areas, where you’ll embark on a tour of Millionaire’s Row. These opulent homes along the water’s edge house the wealthiest individuals in the country. At the end of Millionaires Row, we enter the Intracoastal, where your whole-day boat charter in Fort Lauderdale and custom itinerary can begin.

Fort Lauderdale Day Trip Spots

Fort Lauderdale offers a wealth of experiences for those looking to explore the waterways, and when you rent a boat without a captain, the possibilities are endless. Whether you’re seeking relaxation, adventure, or a little bit of both, there’s something for everyone to enjoy on the water. The city’s canals and surrounding coastline provide the perfect setting for sightseeing, thrilling activities, and natural exploration. With the freedom to craft your perfect day on the water, you can choose from a variety of activities that suit your mood, all while enjoying the incredible scenery that Fort Lauderdale is known for.

From cruising along the scenic Intracoastal to exploring underwater worlds or soaking up the sun on local sandbars, there are countless opportunities to make memories. Whether you’re spending a few hours or the whole day on your boat rental, you’ll have the flexibility to design your ideal itinerary. We’ve highlighted a few of the most popular things to do while boating in Fort Lauderdale, offering a glimpse into the experiences that await you on your trip.

Sightsee

If you’re looking to take it easy and explore at a relaxed pace, sightseeing is one of the best ways to spend your day on the water. Fort Lauderdale is home to hundreds of miles of winding waterways, offering endless opportunities to discover everything the city has to offer. Cruise through residential neighborhoods lined with lush greenery and multi-million-dollar mansions, or float past some of the city’s most iconic landmarks. Along the way, you’ll pass waterfront parks, bustling marinas, and beautiful estates, each with its own unique charm. For those with a keen eye for history, you can also explore Fort Lauderdale’s past through the canals, where remnants of its historical roots are scattered throughout.

Take a leisurely journey along the Intracoastal Waterway or venture into the nearby mangroves and natural preserves. With each turn, you’ll encounter a new view that could include majestic yachts, wildlife, or charming coastal cottages. No matter where you go, the scenery will captivate you and provide plenty of opportunities for photos and sightseeing.

Adventure

For those seeking a bit of adrenaline, Fort Lauderdale’s waters provide the perfect playground for thrilling activities. Water tubing is a fantastic option for high-speed fun and excitement. Strap in and hold on tight as you zip across the water, feeling the rush of wind and spray as you’re towed behind the boat. Whether you’re flying across the waves or getting airborne with sharp turns, water tubing is guaranteed to provide plenty of excitement for you and your friends. It’s the perfect choice for anyone looking to inject a bit of adventure into their boating experience.

As your friends take turns on the tube, the spacious cockpit provides an ideal vantage point to capture all the action. Snap photos or record videos as you watch your friends soar across the water, creating memories that will last long after the day is over.

Sandbars

One of the most beloved attractions in Fort Lauderdale is the Fort Lauderdale Sandbar, a shallow, pristine spot located by the Intracoastal Waterway. This popular location is ideal for a range of water activities, including swimming, sunbathing, and relaxing with friends. The sandbar is particularly well-suited for boaters, as it offers a tranquil environment perfect for anchoring up and enjoying the beautiful surroundings. Whether you’re floating in the water or lounging on the boat, this is an essential destination for anyone who loves to spend time on the water and soak up the sun.

For those renting a boat for a longer trip, you have the opportunity to explore even more sandy retreats. Head south to the iconic Haulover Sandbar in Miami, a hotspot known for its lively atmosphere and incredible views, or venture north towards Lake Boca, another picturesque and peaceful spot to relax and enjoy the water. The sandbars offer a welcoming environment for everyone, from those looking for a relaxing day in the sun to those seeking a lively party atmosphere with friends.

Snorkeling

No trip to Fort Lauderdale is complete without exploring the vibrant marine life that surrounds it. South Florida is home to the Florida Reef, the third-largest barrier reef system in the world, which stretches from Fort Lauderdale all the way down to the Keys. This underwater paradise is teeming with biodiversity, making it one of the best places in the world for snorkeling and scuba diving. Rent a boat for the day and take advantage of the area’s incredible proximity to some of the best snorkeling spots in the region.

The reef is home to a wide variety of marine species, including colorful fish, sea turtles, rays, and even the occasional dolphin or manatee. Whether you’re an experienced snorkeler or a beginner, the calm, clear waters of Fort Lauderdale make it an ideal place to explore the ocean’s wonders. You can spend hours cruising to different locations, hopping off the boat to swim among vibrant coral gardens, or simply enjoy the natural beauty of the underwater world.

Whether you’re planning a casual meal with friends, a relaxing lunch on the water, or a romantic dinner cruise for two, we’ve got you covered with a variety of options.

Lunch

If you need a break during your adventures, stop by any popular eating destinations, such as Shooters or 17th Street Fisheries, located right on the water. If you’re still at a sandbar when hunger strikes, you can take advantage of the food boat that frequents the area. Additionally, we offer a lunch package of our own, including hot dogs, and sodas, if you don’t want to make any stops.
