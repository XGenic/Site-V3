What To Do With a No Captain Boat Rental

When you set out to explore the beautiful waterways of Fort Lauderdale, you’ll quickly discover a wealth of exciting activities and breathtaking sights to enjoy. Whether you already have a detailed itinerary mapped out or are looking for inspiration to enhance your adventure, there’s something for everyone to experience on the water.

To help you make the most of your day, we’ve highlighted some of the top activities and destinations that are perfect for a boat rental in Fort Lauderdale without a captain. From serene cruising and scenic spots to fun water activities, these suggestions are designed to ensure your time on the water is as memorable and enjoyable as possible. Keep reading for ideas to make your Fort Lauderdale boating adventure truly unforgettable!

Sandbars

The sandbars in South Florida are popular attractions for locals and tourists alike. This unique and shallow spot is perfect for swimming, sunbathing, relaxing, or having a good ol’ boat party. The natural occurrence, caused by how the tides and currents move in the area, keeps the waters clear and warm, creating a perfect place to hang out and enjoy the beautiful Florida sunshine. Filled daily with boats, jet skis, and other watercraft, it’s a great place to take any of your boat rentals without a captain.

Restaurants

Take advantage of your no-captain boat rental in Fort Lauderdale with a unique dining experience. With the vast waterways being such a vital part of the city’s identity, you may not be surprised at how boat-friendly certain things are. One of these is a selection of well-known restaurants on the water. With their docks, you can tie your boat and step off the vessel directly into the restaurant. If you’d like some recommendations before you head off, don’t hesitate to ask us!

Tour

A significant part of enjoying a boat rental with no captain is deciding when and where you want to go. There are plenty of things to see along the waters of Fort Lauderdale, allowing you to tour at your own pace. One of the most popular locations is, of course, Millionaire’s Row, a stretch of waterfront properties that are owned by some of the wealthiest individuals in the country, with their opulence on full display as you cruise down the water. Going further inland, you’ll meet the city’s center, seeing essential landmarks such as the Stranahan House and Riverside Hotel. Take advantage of the self-guided Fort Lauderdale boat tour and enter Port Everglades, one of the busiest cruise ports in the world.

Atlantic

Those looking to rent a yacht without a captain in Fort Lauderdale should take advantage of the vicinity and explore the vibrant marine life that inhabits nearby waters. South Florida sits at the northern tip of the Florida Reef, the third-largest barrier reef in the world. These reefs are a hotspot for biodiversity and are home to over 6000 species of marine life. You can spend the entire day on the water with abundant wildlife to explore while snorkeling Fort Lauderdale. Even if you don’t intend to go on seabed adventures, it’s an excellent opportunity to bask in the sun and laze around in the water.
