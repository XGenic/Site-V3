Cruising

You can make use of your catamaran charter to cruise through the inland waters of Fort Lauderdale. Glide past the iconic Millionaires Row, where magnificent waterfront mansions and world-class mega-yachts create a stunning panorama of luxury and nautical prestige. Your journey will showcase the architectural marvels of Bahia Mar, a renowned marina that has long been a centerpiece of Fort Lauderdale’s maritime culture. As you navigate these sheltered waters, our expert guides will share fascinating insights about the area’s rich maritime history, pointing out architectural landmarks, celebrity homes, and the intricate network of canals that have made many compare our city to Venice.

Bimini

Discover the magic of Bimini, the westernmost islands of the Bahamas, just a short catamaran charter from Fort Lauderdale. This tropical paradise offers an intimate escape with its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and rich marine ecosystem. Bimini is a haven for adventure seekers and relaxation enthusiasts alike, boasting world-class sport fishing, incredible snorkeling and diving sites, and fascinating historical landmarks like the legendary “Healing Hole” and remnants of presumed Atlantis-related structures. From swimming with wild dolphins to exploring vibrant coral reefs, enjoying local Bahamian cuisine, or simply lounging on powdery white sands, Bimini provides an extraordinary mix of natural beauty, marine life, and cultural experiences that make every moment of your Fort Lauderdale to Bahamas charter truly unforgettable.
