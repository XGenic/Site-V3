Formula 40 PC — High-Performance Cruiser

Take on the waters of Fort Lauderdale with our Formula 40 PC yacht charter. This high-performance cruiser offers the perfect balance of speed, comfort, and versatility, making it ideal for a variety of on-the-water experiences. Whether you’re looking to explore the scenic intercoastal, anchor at a sandbar for a fun-filled afternoon, or embark on a high-speed thrill ride along the Atlantic coast, the Formula 40 PC delivers an unforgettable adventure.

Its sleek design and powerful engine make it a great choice for those who want to maximize their time on the water. For a more relaxed experience, tour through Fort Lauderdale’s iconic waterways, taking in the beautiful skyline and waterfront mansions. The spacious cockpit and wraparound seating provide ample space for lounging, while the shaded hardtop ensures comfort even on the sunniest days.
