Fountaine Pajot Sonya 57 — Sailing Catamaran

Take on the waters of Fort Lauderdale with our Fountaine Pajot yacht charter. This sailing yacht is more than capable of doing it all, ensuring that your day on the water will be one that you soon won’t forget. For those who want to lounge on the water, a trip through the intercoastal is a beautiful way to see Fort Lauderdale and the surrounding areas from a point of view that isn’t typically seen. You can finish the day off with a private dinner cruise on the yacht or choose to stop by one of our popular waterfront restaurants.

For noteworthy occasions and celebrations, the Fountaine can be a more than capable venue for any event planned. Whether it’s a birthday or a bachelorette party before the big day, it’s hard to beat the beautiful waters of Fort Lauderdale as the backdrop to your special day.
