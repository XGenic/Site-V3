Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin — Premium Day Cruiser

The Axopar 37 XC Cross Cabin is a sleek and sophisticated vessel that combines luxury with versatility on the waters of Fort Lauderdale. At 37 feet, this premium day cruiser offers a perfect balance of indoor and outdoor space. It features a revolutionary cross-cabin design that provides both weather protection and an immersive boating experience.

Cruising through the waters of Ft. Lauderdale becomes an elevated experience aboard the Axopar 37 XC. Whether navigating the New River’s winding channels, exploring Millionaire’s Row, or stopping at a sandbar, this vessel delivers exceptional performance and comfort. Its advanced hull design ensures a smooth ride even in choppy conditions.
