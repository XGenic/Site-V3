Chris-Craft Catalina 26 — Center Console Day Cruiser

Take on the waters of Fort Lauderdale with our Chris-Craft Catalina 26, a center console day cruiser that embodies timeless style, unmatched craftsmanship, and agile performance. Known for its iconic American heritage, Chris-Craft delivers a yachting experience that turns heads at the dock and performs with confidence offshore. From its hand-finished teak accents to its Midnight Blue hull and rich ivory upholstery, every detail speaks to decades of refined boatbuilding.

This yacht is as photogenic as it is practical, making it an ideal choice for elevated leisure on the water. The Catalina 26’s deep-V hull and powerful outboard setup make it perfect for zipping between sandbars, waterfront restaurants, and scenic stretches of the Intracoastal, all while riding in unmistakable luxury.
