Starcraft Marine Pontoon EX — Sandbar Pontoon

For those looking for a simple and accessible day on the water with friends, there are few better boats to choose than a pontoon. With plenty of space to move around it, it’s the perfect vessel for large parties.

Capable of going in shallow waters, this pontoon is the perfect choice to visit any of our local sandbars, the perfect spot for a party atmosphere on the water. The integrated swim ladder makes it easy to get off the boat and into the water and back on just as easily!
