Savannah 54 — Express Cruiser

Step aboard the Savannah 54, a beautifully crafted express cruiser that blends refined style with spacious comfort, creating a perfect setting for both entertaining and relaxation on the water. With its wide beam and intelligently designed layout, this 54-foot yacht delivers the feel of a much larger vessel, ideal for those seeking a roomy, comfortable, and elegant escape along Fort Lauderdale’s scenic waterways.

The Savannah 54 features a striking exterior and a thoughtfully appointed interior that balances form and function. From its full-beam salon with a retractable roof to its seamless indoor-outdoor flow, every inch is designed to make your time onboard effortless and enjoyable. Whether you’re lounging with friends or hosting a small gathering, the Savannah 54 provides the space and comfort to do it all with ease.
