We will be doing a sweep of relevent information for the service pages, by putting in the correct boats availble as well as sidebar information

## BOATS
- You will be modifying the "boats" in the window.servicePageConfig section of each service page
- For each boat, you will reuse the same information, price, picture etc.
- You can pull all the relevent information from boats in the individual yacht pages
- Many times the included boats will be referenced by tier instead of individual boats, you can find the tier of the boat in the Yachts page

## CALANDAR
- You will replace the temp calandar link in the sidebar with the correct one for that page

## NO SIDEBAR
- You will need to create a different structure for pages in which we do no need a sidebar. All you need to do is not display the sidebar or any of its sidebar for the relevent pages. You can just extend the left panel all the way
